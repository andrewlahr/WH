Daily streamflow and brown trout population processes in regulated Montana rivers

What this is

Analysis supporting Bureau of Reclamation flow-management decisions on the Madison River and other regulated Montana streams. It estimates, for every day of the calendar year, how streamflow affects brown trout recruitment, adult survival, and total population production.

Three questions:

  1. When does releasing water do the most good? Guidance for water managers, irrigators, and holders of leased water rights.
  2. What do daily-scale results say about trout population ecology in Montana? Which vital rate is flow-limited, and in which season.
  3. How do the answers change under Bureau of Reclamation forecasted flows?

Headline

Blocked cross-validated \(R^2\) for the daily curve (fpc) was -0.207 (permutation \(p\) = 0.364); the four-window confirmatory model gave -0.433.

No flow signal survives validation. Results are reported as a null. Curves are shown for completeness and labelled unsupported throughout.
